About

I'm an Artificial Intelligence student at FPT University (GPA 3.6/4.0) who enjoys turning LLM research into things people actually use. My recent work is on GraphRAG for long documents, where I combine structure-aware chunking with knowledge-graph retrieval, and on agentic chatbots built on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore.

I care about measuring before choosing: every pipeline I build is benchmarked with RAGAS and ablations. Outside of LLMs, I've also shipped an autonomous driving stack on an NVIDIA Jetson Nano that won first place at FPT University's Jetson AI Racer Challenge.

Projects

Feb – Jun 2026 · Research

MD-GraphRAG ↗

Paper accepted · EAI FISAT 2026

Markdown-aware GraphRAG for long-document question answering. Structure-preserving chunking plus two-channel retrieval (vector search + knowledge-graph traversal), with KG triplets extracted by local Llama 3.1 and Qwen 2.5.

  • Benchmarked 4 pipeline variants on 7 QA datasets (HotpotQA, QASPER, PubMedQA…) with RAGAS.
  • Vector stores: ChromaDB, pgvector with BGE-M3 embeddings.
